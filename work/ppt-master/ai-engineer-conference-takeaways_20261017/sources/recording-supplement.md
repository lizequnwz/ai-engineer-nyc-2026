# AI Engineer NYC 2026 — recording-enhanced learning report

**Updated October 25, 2026 · For data scientists, AI engineers and technical leads**

The central lesson is to engineer a complete, reviewable work product: the right evidence, the right professional method, an explicit acceptance decision and controlled execution. The newly available material makes that lesson concrete through wealth planning, investment research, credit operations, accounting, fraud modeling and software delivery.

## Six lessons to teach the team

### 1. Build three complementary evaluation views

An acceptable result, an acceptable process and an authorized state transition are separate requirements. A report can contain the right total while using stale facts; an accounting agent can make a reconciliation tie by inventing an adjustment; a sound draft can still be sent without permission. Keep diagnostic scores, complete-task acceptance and critical blockers separate.

**Try this:** For one historical task, retain its original data and allowed tools. Add a missing-evidence variant and a tempting prohibited shortcut. Score the final artifact, the consequential decisions, and the action boundary. Record not-applicable behavior separately from compliance.

**Evidence:** [Basis](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/basis) · [Vals AI](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/vals) · [Coinbase](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/coinbase) · [Arize · Sofia Jakovcevic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v5tm5EAzsAs) · [Air Canada · Nathanael Weill](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c_SPW__jF9w)

### 2. Treat numerical grounding as a chain of checks

Retrieval relevance does not establish financial meaning. Select the eligible source, resolve the correct fact, retain units and period, compute reproducibly, and validate the final output. A citation attached after generation cannot repair a wrong entity, superseded filing or unsupported calculation.

**Try this:** Use a typed claim containing entity, metric, period, unit/currency, source version, as-of cutoff and derivation. Test date parsing, negative signs, nulls, join coverage and evidence survival after export. This combined contract is our synthesis rather than one firm's disclosed architecture.

**Evidence:** [Bridgewater](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/bridgewater) · [Kepler · October 14 follow-up](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/kepler-14) · [Kepler · attended breakout release](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dZgA6btnb_s) · [Southbridge](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/southbridge) · [OpenAI](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/openai) · [Compound AI](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/compound)

### 3. Give memory time, ownership and a correction policy

Memory is useful when it preserves reusable procedure and current evidence. It is harmful when it carries old amounts, unexamined rules or injected instructions into the next task. Task checkpoints, professional know-how and changing business beliefs have different lifetimes.

**Try this:** Advance a test business one period: change the amounts and one procedure. Require fresh facts and revalidation of the changed rule. Store source time, correction history and sensor health. Missing observations remain unknown. Freeze historical snapshots so later outcomes cannot leak backward.

**Evidence:** [Ramp · Ryan Stevens written follow-up](https://dev.to/dailycontext/building-better-agents-through-end-to-end-evals-pgh) · [Citadel Securities](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/citadel) · [Arize · Aparna Dhinakaran](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/arize) · [Bloomberg](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/bloomberg)

### 4. Make long-running work durable and authority explicit

These examples separate persistent progress from bounded executions, waiting states and human handoffs. Perpetual shows one design in which deterministic orchestration owns progress and the model supplies bounded judgments. A valid schema does not by itself authorize an external action.

**Try this:** Record the approved plan version, exact payload, next event, deadline and owner. Distinguish failed from outcome-unknown external actions; inspect the latter before retrying. Test that revocation, tenant boundaries, budgets and required approvals are enforced outside the model.

**Evidence:** [Arca](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/arca) · [Perpetual](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/perpetual) · [Coinbase](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/coinbase) · [Two Sigma](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/two-sigma) · [Intuit Credit Karma](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/credit-karma) · [Bloomberg](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/bloomberg)

### 5. Measure reviewed work and preserve replaceable implementations

Leading examples put proprietary data, professional methods, evaluation assets and operational controls around increasingly interchangeable models. The same approach can support financial preparation, transaction modeling, software modernization and credit operations, but each has a different accepted work product and latency budget.

**Try this:** Compare equivalent completed work at agreed quality. Include unsuccessful attempts, review, rework, tools and support. Preserve the task definitions and scorecards when changing models or scaffolding. A lower token bill, adoption count or faster first draft is only one part of the result.

**Evidence:** [Coatue](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/coatue) · [Blackstone / Norm.ai](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/blackstone-norm) · [Apollo / Redding Ridge / Rowspace](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/apollo-rowspace) · [Wells Fargo](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/wells-fargo) · [Jellyfish](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/jellyfish) · [Contour Asset Management](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZMZlAn6Pyfg)

### 6. Grow by discovering the bottleneck and proving the repair

The engineer's durable responsibility is to understand the work, define good completion, instrument execution, diagnose failures and verify changes. Faster code creation can shift the constraint to review, operations or security. Making checks cheaper and easier is therefore part of product engineering.

**Try this:** Own one complete improvement cycle: interview the practitioner, write an acceptance contract, inspect a failure trace, test one repair, examine paired regressions and review the current exported artifact. Retain the learning so the next engineer starts from evidence.

**Evidence:** [Arize · Laurie Voss](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kspZP5BgkEU) · [Capital One](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/capital-one) · [Arize · Aparna Dhinakaran](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/arize) · [Sonar](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/sonar) · [Monzo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=adfw3xJBx1o) · [Prime Intellect](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/prime-intellect)

## Worked example: a correct-looking answer can fail three ways

This example is invented. A reviewer requests a current monthly cash-flow snapshot. Approved income is $8,000; current expenses are $6,000; superseded expenses are $5,500. The task permits a draft only.

| Candidate | Arithmetic | Evidence / behavior | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| $8,000 − $5,500 = $2,500 | Correct on stated operands | Uses superseded expenses | Reject current-source validity |
| $8,000 − $6,000 = $2,000 | Correct and reconstructable | Uses current facts; flags missing insurance information | Eligible for professional draft review |
| Edits a record so the books tie | May make the final total match | Unsupported intervention | Reject process adherence |
| Sends a correct draft without approval | Correct contents possible | Exceeds the permitted action | Block execution |

The lesson is not to replace exact checks with a judge. Use exact checks where possible, an expert rubric for substantive quality, a behavior check for consequential decisions, and an enforcing service for action authority. Human approval of a draft and permission to execute are separate events.

## Company stories, grouped by method

### Grounding and memory

#### Bridgewater

Vault joins time- and source-aware retrieval with an investor workbench and feedback into the research corpus. The transcript adds a roughly 200-question labeled relevance set and a reported precision@10 improvement from about 60% to 90–95%.

**Transferable practice:** Test eligible-source coverage separately from human-facing relevance; include plausible negatives and restrictive filters. Preserve information cutoff and access scope before synthesis.

**Boundary:** Internal retrieval results, without a disclosed held-out design or investment-performance estimate. The speaker corrects 'recall' to precision; the intended product direction is reactive resource to proactive participant.

**Source:** [Organizer transcript — Vault: Making Unstructured Data Work for Investing](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/bridgewater) · [Timestamped recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akEqiXFo3GM&t=442s)

#### Kepler · October 14 follow-up

Separate source selection, exact fact retrieval and arithmetic. Models plan with fact identifiers while code resolves facts and computes; direct reads and search both respect access boundaries. Final post-processing is still needed because generation can invent a number afterward.

**Transferable practice:** Check entity, period, unit, source version, calculation and final rendered claim separately. Unsupported claims must be removed, corrected or escalated.

**Boundary:** No zero-hallucination guarantee follows from pointer lookup. This main-stage follow-up is distinct from the attended 'Do Not Let Models Invent Numbers' breakout.

**Source:** [Organizer transcript — Why Wall Street Still Doesn’t Trust AI](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/kepler-14) · [Timestamped recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jiUC6IUMdvc&t=20975s)

#### Southbridge

Observation, storage, decision definitions and model assumptions impose different schemas. Examples retain row counts while dates disappear, categories change or negative signs lose meaning. Multiple models can share the same wrong convention.

**Transferable practice:** Version decision-specific definitions and test typed intermediate fields: signs, nulls, dates, categories, grain and join coverage. Agreement is a diagnostic signal, not independent truth.

**Boundary:** Task examples support semantic checks, not a universal claim that schemas or ensembles always succeed or fail.

**Source:** [Organizer transcript — Just-in-Time Ontologies](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/southbridge) · [Timestamped recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akEqiXFo3GM&t=14548s)

#### Citadel Securities

Institutional memory updates and ages beliefs, observes contradictions, grades past predictions and accepts corrections. Reported status and evidence-based status remain separate; historical snapshots are preserved. Models propose updates, while code and people commit them.

**Transferable practice:** Store evidence time, origin, source health and correction history. Missing telemetry is unknown, not zero. Backtest as-of snapshots against later observations without rewriting history.

**Boundary:** The reported associations are observational. Client-franchise expansion is a direction, not an established completed capability.

**Source:** [Organizer transcript — Your Company Has No Model of Itself: Bootstrapping a World Model from Slack, Decks and Meetings](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/citadel) · [Timestamped recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jiUC6IUMdvc&t=23913s)

**Transferable practice:** Test the exported document as well as the chat response: values, periods, units, provenance and corrections must survive into the artifact.

**Boundary:** Product description and forward-looking agent capabilities do not establish universal availability, entitlements or measured financial accuracy. Uncertain provider spelling in the attendee notes is not guessed.

### Authority and reliable workflows

#### Two Sigma

An agent registry makes ownership, purpose, tools, resources and risk visible. Scoped access and a separate approval path constrain execution; approval triage is a different responsibility from enforcement.

**Transferable practice:** Test a requested action at the enforcing boundary, including revocation, cross-user access and duplicate requests. Record which component can actually deny it.

**Boundary:** A described control framework is not a measured protection rate or proof that a triage classifier can waive required approval.

**Source:** [Organizer transcript — Balancing Acceleration and Safety for AI Agents](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/two-sigma) · [Timestamped recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akEqiXFo3GM&t=1435s)

#### Perpetual

Language interpretation produces typed intent. A compiler validates a closed action vocabulary and can request clarification or refuse. Approved plans are immutable; approval binds the exact outgoing payload. An unknown side-effect outcome goes to manual review rather than blind retry.

**Transferable practice:** Distinguish failed, succeeded and outcome-unknown actions. Test changed payloads after approval and interruptions around external writes.

**Boundary:** Typed validity does not establish semantic correctness. Local-model routing examples are not measured fleetwide parity; the training feedback loop is described as future work.

**Boundary:** Framework and examples, not evidence that every illustrated process runs autonomously at Coinbase. Thresholds shown are not portable safety constants.

**Source:** [Organizer transcript — Trusting Agents in Policy-Driven Workflows](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/coinbase) · [Timestamped recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jiUC6IUMdvc&t=3124s)

**Source:** [Organizer transcript — Paving the Road to Production: Governed Enterprise AI Agents](https://ai.engineer/nyc/2026/talks/credit-karma) · [Timestamped recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jiUC6IUMdvc&t=14322s)

#### Basis

A reconciliation can reach zero variance through an unsupported adjustment and still be wrong. Behavior specs first ask whether a situation occurred, then whether the agent acted appropriately. Forks isolate a decision; cheaper simulations test repair hypotheses.

**Boundary:** Agent explanations are clues, not proven causes. The fork is an illustrated repair, not a measured general success rate; outcome evaluations remain necessary.

### AI engineer growth

#### Arize · Aparna Dhinakaran

A disposable sandbox receives temporary repository/credential access; issue history, useful skills and run telemetry persist outside it. Replacement runs can resume from durable progress. Human-labeled data calibrates evaluators before use.

**Transferable practice:** Separate the lifetime of task state from the lifetime of execution authority. Trace the agents modifying the system as well as those serving users.

**Boundary:** Proposed fixes/PRs and regression evaluation do not establish safe autonomous production release or measured diagnostic accuracy.

## A compact practitioner playbook

| Stage | Artifact to keep | Check before moving on |
