This is a selected mechanism map rather than a universal architecture or maturity ranking. Bridgewater, Kepler and Contour illuminate evidence and research; Range and the dated Morgan Stanley, UBS and RBC cases illuminate wealth work. Two Sigma and Wise illuminate authority, Monzo, Optiver and Vanguard engineering platforms, and Ramp, Arize, Sonar and Capital One improvement and maintenance. Compare equivalent work before interpreting time, cost or adoption. Architecture describes intended operation and production use describes adoption; neither alone proves effectiveness or causal value. The source HTML contains the expanded twenty-five-firm atlas, while this PowerPoint deliberately shows the selected case map. The examples are uneven self-reports, and company affiliation or a panel appearance does not establish deployment of every discussed technique.
