The ladder separates four increasingly strong claims. Adoption describes who uses a system and for what tasks. Accepted work adds output quality, critical failures and reviewer effort. Process improvement adds time, throughput, capacity and reliability. Attributable value needs a credible alternative, full cost and uncertainty. Contour described roughly three months of AI and non-AI workflow tracking, but random assignment, controls and causal alpha estimates were not captured. That window is an account of the case, not a universal validation period. Preserve point-in-time information, reasoning, reviewer responses and decisions so later market success cannot be reconstructed with hindsight. Report process changes separately from returns influenced by markets, selection and other drivers. Usage can remain high while rework is substantial.
