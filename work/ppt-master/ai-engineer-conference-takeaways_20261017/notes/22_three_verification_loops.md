Sonar’s three verification loops catch different defects. Creation guides goals, standards and constraints and inspects generated work for local issues; another model’s agreement or a quick human approval is not independent correctness evidence. Release tests interfaces, permissions, financial invariants and broader regressions while retaining reproducible evidence of what the checker detects. Operation reproduces escaped failures and verifies repairs against other work, preserving rollback conditions. Prevention remains a priority alongside detection and response. Intent, guardrails, verification and evidence form a useful checklist; their conceptual product has no captured calibrated scale or probabilistic interpretation. The three loops come from the official abstract and the four factors from notes. The code-size example lacks benchmark conditions, so neither a universal fifty-thousand-line limit nor autonomous self-healing is established.
