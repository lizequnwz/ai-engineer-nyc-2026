Vanguard’s agenda-only ledger-modernization story keeps financial invariants central. The advertised structure separates producer onboarding and migration, replicated gold-copy state with change-data-capture synchronization, and decoupled consumer access and migration using GraphQL. Observe no missing or duplicate transactions, consistent state, database idempotency, controlled retries and recovery, and reviewable reconciliation at the relevant boundaries. Faster code generation cannot demonstrate those properties. The abstract says AI helped maintain two codebases, but it does not isolate the incremental productivity, quality or risk effect. Compare equivalent changes, correctness, review and rework, and operating cost before attributing the modernization outcome to AI. The case is neither an autonomous financial agent nor a prescription to introduce dual systems into another product.
