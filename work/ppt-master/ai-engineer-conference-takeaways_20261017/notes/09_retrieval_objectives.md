Vault’s human and agent retrieval contrast is a useful workload heuristic. People often need compact, high-signal results because attention is scarce, while agents may require broader recall to avoid missing decisive evidence. Both still need relevance, permissions, meaning and freshness. Measure whether the required evidence is found before assessing whether compression preserves it. More context or more retrieved documents do not establish better answers. The reported investor workbench includes activity feeds, evidence-backed summaries and a what-am-I-missing surface. Evaluate relevance, omissions, timeliness and review time against the existing information flow.
