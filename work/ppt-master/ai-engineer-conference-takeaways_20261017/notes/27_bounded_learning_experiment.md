A small complete experiment can develop the full discipline. Choose a research brief, analytical result or preparation draft with clear users, acceptance and approved or synthetic inputs. Record the current quality, time, cost and human effort before selecting a preferred model. Change one skill bundle, memory rule or model while holding snapshots and assessment cases fixed; separate tuning and include ordinary, ambiguous and consequential-failure cases. The deliverable is an evidence card covering task, baseline, deliberate change, failures, quality, effort, cost, limits and the next decision. Share one reproducible case with a peer and state what would change the conclusion. A deterministic solution, inadequate data or retiring the approach can all be useful outcomes. This exercise is not production approval or sufficient statistical validation.
