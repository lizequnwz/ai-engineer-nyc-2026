Release logic should separate noncompensating blockers from graded usefulness. Depending on the domain, blockers may include unauthorized access or actions, unsupported consequential numbers or omitted approvals; define them before looking at candidate scores. Review acceptance, paired regressions, judge validity, uncertainty and total effort and cost together. The evidence packet retains intended scope, system and evaluator versions, results by slice, traces, regressions, expert disagreements, known limits and accepted exceptions. A named owner decides whether to investigate or promote within that scope. Monitor with explicit pause, rollback and renewed-review conditions, broadening assessment when uncertainty could change the judgment. A clean finite suite does not prove zero risk. This is our proposed gate, not an implemented bank threshold or rollback policy captured at the conference.
