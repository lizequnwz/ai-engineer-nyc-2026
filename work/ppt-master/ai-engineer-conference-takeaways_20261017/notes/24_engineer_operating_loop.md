An AI engineer’s responsibility continues after the first working feature. Frame accepted work, build one complete slice, learn from traces, cases and failures, then verify, release and observe. Capture request, context, intermediate actions, result, evaluation and versions so a trace can explain why a workflow failed. Arize’s diagnosis account and Ramp’s task-level ablations motivate this habit rather than validating autonomous repair. Build a portfolio of reproducible failures, baseline comparisons, specific repairs, release decisions and observed tradeoffs. Keep issue discovery, a proposed fix, validation and release authority separate. Stronger engineering judgment becomes visible through reviewable decisions; the number of frameworks or tools someone knows does not establish diagnostic accuracy or a reliable improvement loop.
