Kepler’s account separates source retrieval, code-based calculation, typed facts, citation metadata and support repair or filtering. Code can make arithmetic reproducible, but cannot establish that an input represents the intended entity, period or definition. The proposed typed-fact contract therefore carries entity, metric, period, value, unit and currency, document version and passage, and calculation inputs and transformations. These extended semantic checks were not confirmed as Kepler coverage, and the slide does not claim to reproduce a complete ordered architecture. Citation repair can find a formatting match while leaving the financial interpretation wrong. If support still fails, remove the claim, abstain or escalate. Removing a citation while keeping an unsupported sentence is not a trustworthy repair. Rough retrieval settings in the notes are not defaults.
