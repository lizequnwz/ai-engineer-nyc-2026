The five responsibilities reinforce one another on a real workflow. Domain translation turns professional judgment into required inputs, questions and acceptance conditions. Data reasoning connects that judgment to entities, time, units, definitions and transformations. System design draws ownership, allowed actions, state and exceptions; evaluation challenges the workflow and its judge using critical failures, task success and full cost. Evidence communication explains task, mechanism, comparison, result and uncertainty together, distinguishing deployment from effectiveness and useful output from causal value. Develop complementary fluency through shared work rather than requiring every person to become the sole expert in every area. This is our proposed competency framework, not a hiring survey or a validated career model attributed to a conference company.
