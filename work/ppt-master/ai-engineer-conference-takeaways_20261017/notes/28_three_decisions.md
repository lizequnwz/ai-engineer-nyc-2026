The practical return from the conference is three explicit decisions and a bounded comparison. Work identifies the person whose accepted output matters and the bottleneck to improve before discussing tools. Boundary defines sources, information cutoff, calculations, permitted actions, retained human judgment, blockers and the response to missing evidence. Evidence specifies a comparison with the existing process on equivalent work and agreed acceptance, including critical failures, every attempt, review effort and full cost. Agree in advance what supports continuing, revising or retiring. The value of a report-back is a judgment peers can assess with its uncertainty visible. Conference mechanisms alone do not establish internal readiness, and this briefing makes no Fidelity project commitment; ownership, scope and timing remain explicit discussion decisions.
