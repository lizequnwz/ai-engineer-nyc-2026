# 01_dependable_work

The central question is what must surround a model for a professional to accept useful financial work. This briefing consolidates research and twelve identified attendee-note sets from AI Engineer NYC on October thirteenth and fourteenth, twenty twenty-six. The five chapters connect strategy, grounded evidence, evaluation, reliable workflows and engineer growth into one operating argument. The company stories provide mechanisms to examine, not a ranking of firms or independently verified benefits. Attendee accounts, agenda-only context, dated public disclosures and our proposed methods retain different evidence boundaries. The core presentation is designed for forty minutes plus discussion, with detailed slides and notes for later independent review.

---

# 02_five_disciplines

Dependable financial AI is a complete job, not a model in isolation. Begin by defining valuable accepted work and credible economics. Ground that work in sources, meaning, time, versions and access; evaluate the task, judge, answer selection and release; then encode expert methods and enforce authority through reliable workflows. The engineer owns the improvement loop across all five responsibilities. Bridgewater’s information problem, Range’s planning method and wealth preparation illustrate distinct bottlenecks. Name the accepted output and scarce resource before choosing a tool. Trace definitions, actions and corrections through the final artifact, and use Ramp’s ablations and Arize’s diagnosis ideas to make one bounded, reviewable comparison.

# 06_value_ladder

The ladder separates four increasingly strong claims. Adoption describes who uses a system and for what tasks. Accepted work adds output quality, critical failures and reviewer effort. Process improvement adds time, throughput, capacity and reliability. Attributable value needs a credible alternative, full cost and uncertainty. Contour described roughly three months of AI and non-AI workflow tracking, but random assignment, controls and causal alpha estimates were not captured. That window is an account of the case, not a universal validation period. Preserve point-in-time information, reasoning, reviewer responses and decisions so later market success cannot be reconstructed with hindsight. Report process changes separately from returns influenced by markets, selection and other drivers. Usage can remain high while rework is substantial.

# 07_accepted_work_economics

Evaluate the cost of completed accepted work rather than the price of one generated response. The proposed numerator includes inference, data, tool calls, retries, candidate selection, human review, rework and operational support. Failed and abandoned attempts still consume resources. Compare equivalent workloads at the same acceptance standard and critical limits, because a cheaper model can impose more correction effort. Optiver’s agenda-based task certification suggests quality qualification before price optimization, but qualification on one workload does not establish readiness for another. Predeclare eligible tasks, acceptance and exclusions, and report accepted-task rate beside cost per accepted task. Otherwise narrowing the supported population can create an apparent economic improvement. Neither the sources nor this formula establishes savings or an optimal routing policy.

# 09_retrieval_objectives

Vault’s human and agent retrieval contrast is a useful workload heuristic. People often need compact, high-signal results because attention is scarce, while agents may require broader recall to avoid missing decisive evidence. Both still need relevance, permissions, meaning and freshness. Measure whether the required evidence is found before assessing whether compression preserves it. More context or more retrieved documents do not establish better answers. The reported investor workbench includes activity feeds, evidence-backed summaries and a what-am-I-missing surface. Evaluate relevance, omissions, timeliness and review time against the existing information flow. The notes establish no optimal retrieval setting, changed habits, predictive accuracy or investment returns, and agents cannot simply ignore precision.

# 12_inspectable_artifact

# 15_judges_and_selection

The grader and deployed answer selector are separate evaluated systems. Compare judge decisions with independently reviewed and adjudicated examples, retaining serious false passes even when average agreement is high. A false failure loses useful work and creates review burden, so a stricter judge is not automatically better. The synthetic fail, pass, fail strip contains a usable second candidate, but it is one teaching case rather than an empirical pass-at-three estimate. Someone still has to recognize and select that answer. Pass-at-k asks whether at least one of k candidates meets the specified test; state workload, test, k and first-attempt behavior, then evaluate the actual verifier or selector under that budget. Count retries, tools, verification, latency and human review. Upstart calibration is program context, and its self-improvement loops remained under development.

# 16_paired_ablations

Ablations hold the work and assessment conditions fixed while changing one component. Replay matched tasks with the same snapshots, permissions, rubric and evaluator versions, and attempt policy. Compare task and evidence quality, critical defects, human effort, cost and latency rather than only one average score. Repeat stochastic assessments enough to examine uncertainty and important paired failures. Ramp’s notes described reducing eight skills to four, shrinking an Excel skill and trajectory-distilled memory derived from prior runs. No effect sizes, repeated-run results or reproducible construction details were captured, so those choices are examples rather than defaults. Convert observed failures into regression cases while retaining separate ordinary and difficult assessment cases. A component earns its place through agreed tradeoffs, not an intuition that more memory or less context must help.

# 17_release_decision

Release logic should separate noncompensating blockers from graded usefulness. Depending on the domain, blockers may include unauthorized access or actions, unsupported consequential numbers or omitted approvals; define them before looking at candidate scores. Review acceptance, paired regressions, judge validity, uncertainty and total effort and cost together. The evidence packet retains intended scope, system and evaluator versions, results by slice, traces, regressions, expert disagreements, known limits and accepted exceptions. A named owner decides whether to investigate or promote within that scope. Monitor with explicit pause, rollback and renewed-review conditions, broadening assessment when uncertainty could change the judgment. A clean finite suite does not prove zero risk. This is our proposed gate, not an implemented bank threshold or rollback policy captured at the conference.

# 18_controlled_improvement

A failure becomes useful when it can be reproduced and turned into a reviewed experiment. Capture the request, available sources and permissions, tool interactions, output, human correction and relevant versions under existing trace-access rules. Adjudicate whether the defect concerns evidence, reasoning, runtime behavior or the evaluator. Create a versioned case, propose a specific repair, and test it against regressions plus separately retained assessment cases. Diagnosis is a hypothesis; accountable review authorizes a scoped release. Monitor escaped failures, corrections, drift and operating costs afterward. Arize’s account described trace-based issue discovery without measured diagnostic accuracy or demonstrated autonomous remediation. Upstart’s announced improvement loops were being built. More telemetry is not permission to expose sensitive trace content or automatically change production behavior.

# 24_engineer_operating_loop

An AI engineer’s responsibility continues after the first working feature. Frame accepted work, build one complete slice, learn from traces, cases and failures, then verify, release and observe. Capture request, context, intermediate actions, result, evaluation and versions so a trace can explain why a workflow failed. Arize’s diagnosis account and Ramp’s task-level ablations motivate this habit rather than validating autonomous repair. Build a portfolio of reproducible failures, baseline comparisons, specific repairs, release decisions and observed tradeoffs. Keep issue discovery, a proposed fix, validation and release authority separate. Stronger engineering judgment becomes visible through reviewable decisions; the number of frameworks or tools someone knows does not establish diagnostic accuracy or a reliable improvement loop.

# 25_connected_fluency

The five responsibilities reinforce one another on a real workflow. Domain translation turns professional judgment into required inputs, questions and acceptance conditions. Data reasoning connects that judgment to entities, time, units, definitions and transformations. System design draws ownership, allowed actions, state and exceptions; evaluation challenges the workflow and its judge using critical failures, task success and full cost. Evidence communication explains task, mechanism, comparison, result and uncertainty together, distinguishing deployment from effectiveness and useful output from causal value. Develop complementary fluency through shared work rather than requiring every person to become the sole expert in every area. This is our proposed competency framework, not a hiring survey or a validated career model attributed to a conference company.

# 26_lifespan_and_consequence

Expected lifespan and failure consequence are independent design questions. Short-lived work with bounded consequence can be simple and removable with clear scope, ownership and a retirement condition. Long-lived work adds stable interfaces, documentation and regression responsibilities. A short-lived high-consequence tool still needs access, numerical and execution checks, while a durable high-consequence system needs operated controls, recovery and continuing validation. Capital One’s conceptual durability discussion does not establish deployed disposable software or zero replacement cost. Cheap regeneration changes maintenance economics without removing the consequences of a wrong result. When an experiment gains users or dependencies, make an explicit maintenance decision about support, recovery and coverage rather than quietly allowing it to become infrastructure.

# 27_bounded_learning_experiment

A small complete experiment can develop the full discipline. Choose a research brief, analytical result or preparation draft with clear users, acceptance and approved or synthetic inputs. Record the current quality, time, cost and human effort before selecting a preferred model. Change one skill bundle, memory rule or model while holding snapshots and assessment cases fixed; separate tuning and include ordinary, ambiguous and consequential-failure cases. The deliverable is an evidence card covering task, baseline, deliberate change, failures, quality, effort, cost, limits and the next decision. Share one reproducible case with a peer and state what would change the conclusion. A deterministic solution, inadequate data or retiring the approach can all be useful outcomes. This exercise is not production approval or sufficient statistical validation.

# 29_discussion

Use discussion to identify a concrete workflow and an uncertainty that matters. First ask whose accepted work improves and whether faster generation actually saves total effort after review. Next name an unsupported fact, wrong period, unauthorized action or bypassed denial that could escape ordinary review. Correct refusal is a control success, and the judge or reviewer also needs testing. Finally agree which baseline, output checks, cost records or missing prerequisite could change the decision. Record one candidate experiment and one unresolved evidence question. A useful discussion can conclude that prerequisites are missing or that the team should not proceed. Avoid turning the conversation into a tool preference debate or forcing consensus and an immediate commitment.

# 34_passk_and_deployment

Pass-at-k asks a benchmark question: did at least one of k sampled candidates meet the specified test, summarized over tasks? State the workload, test and attempt budget. Ground-truth recognition can behave like an oracle and does not demonstrate that the first candidate succeeds or the operational selector finds the usable one. Run the actual verifier, ranker or human-review process and measure chosen-output acceptance, missed defects, rejected useful candidates and first-attempt behavior while enforcing critical conditions. Include all candidates, tool calls, verification, review and latency when comparing budgets. More attempts can create opportunity while increasing cost and exposure. Keep partial-credit task quality, pass-at-k and selected-output reliability distinct. Chen and colleagues supply this metric and selection distinction, not a finance performance result or a general retry policy.

# 35_measurement_contract

Use acceptance rate and full cost per accepted task together. Acceptance rate divides accepted outputs by eligible assigned tasks and shows rejected, excluded and abandoned outcomes. Unit cost divides total workflow cost, including failed attempts, verification, retries, review and rework, by accepted tasks. Capacity compares accepted work per professional effort or time at equivalent quality and task mix, including preparation, correction, evidence checking, approval and follow-up. Business impact needs an additional credible alternative and attention to selection and other drivers; faster accepted work is a process result rather than automatically causal revenue or investment return. Contour’s roughly three-month tracking account lacks captured sample, assignment, controls and causal estimates. It motivates the comparison but does not define a universal validation window or a verified benefit.
