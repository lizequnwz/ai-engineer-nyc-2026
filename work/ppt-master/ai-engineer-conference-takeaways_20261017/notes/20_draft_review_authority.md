The fictional annual review packet separates arithmetic, professional judgment and execution authority. Every amount is invented: income of one hundred twenty thousand dollars minus recorded expenses of eighty-four thousand gives an arithmetic difference of thirty-six thousand. That is neither disposable capacity nor an insurance need or suitability conclusion. The agent should expose facts, calculations, assumptions, gaps and proposed conclusions for the professional to review. Range’s parallel-drafting vignette motivates measuring accepted work after correction rather than draft volume. Wise’s agenda separates model proposals from authority to act. Accepting or editing a draft does not authorize a send or transaction; a separate service checks user, case and action permission and records approval. This is a proposed workflow, not Range’s disclosed architecture or financial advice.
