Evaluate the cost of completed accepted work rather than the price of one generated response. The proposed numerator includes inference, data, tool calls, retries, candidate selection, human review, rework and operational support. Failed and abandoned attempts still consume resources. Compare equivalent workloads at the same acceptance standard and critical limits, because a cheaper model can impose more correction effort. Optiver’s agenda-based task certification suggests quality qualification before price optimization, but qualification on one workload does not establish readiness for another. Predeclare eligible tasks, acceptance and exclusions, and report accepted-task rate beside cost per accepted task. Otherwise narrowing the supported population can create an apparent economic improvement. Neither the sources nor this formula establishes savings or an optimal routing policy.
