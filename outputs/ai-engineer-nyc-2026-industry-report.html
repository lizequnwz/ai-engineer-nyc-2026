01

Build around a complete, reviewable workflow rather than an impressive answer

Institutional information, procedural knowledge and the final work product belong together. Bridgewater's task-specific information foundation, the finance-interface discussion and the Range skills vignette point toward workflows such as a prepared meeting or a draft analysis that a professional can inspect and accept. The useful success definition includes the evidence, necessary questions, limits, edits and handoff—not just fluent generation.

02

Confidence needs preventive boundaries, independent checks and an evidence trail

Two Sigma's declared access and active controls, Kepler's source/calculation/citation path and Sonar's intent–guardrails–verification–evidence framework reinforce one operating principle: a person or another model agreeing with an answer is insufficient assurance. Make permitted behavior explicit, prevent defined invalid actions, and retain reviewable evidence of the checks performed.

03

Evaluate complete work across quality dimensions, then measure accepted value against a baseline

The end-to-end-evals notes, the banking session’s quality-vector framing, Sonar's judge-variability concern and Day 1's risk/value discussions argue for separate task, safety and experience measures. Inspect evaluator errors, preserve severe-failure gates, and include human review and repeated-attempt cost before interpreting productivity or business value.

Information access and delivery should match the work

The Vault notes distinguish human search, which needs a small high-signal result set, from agent retrieval, which may need broader recall at larger scale.   • Define the user decision and relevant time window before choosing a retrieval approach.
  • Measure retrieval recall for the agent and precision/usefulness of the final human-facing brief separately.
  • Compare a focused brief or feed with on-demand search; count important omissions, irrelevant items and review time.

Limit: The notes do not provide benchmark results, evidence that habits changed, or proof of better predictions. The direction of the reactive/proactive transition is inconsistent in the source.

Match engineering rigor to consequence, dependency and lifespan

The Capital One notes distinguish disposable software used by a few people and replaceable in hours from durable software maintained for years with many dependents.   • Give a short-lived experiment an owner, an explicit expiry/retirement decision and clear limits on use.

Twelve techniques worth bringing back.

These editorial techniques combine learnings from both days with clearly labeled agenda context. They are proposed practices to discuss and test, not results your team has already achieved.

TAKEAWAY 01

Make every financial number traceable

Retain the entity, period, units, currency, source version, and calculation path. A correct citation can still point to the wrong period or denominator. Distinguish sourced figures from calculated results and forecasts.

Attendee notes · S11
TAKEAWAY 02

Separate agent recall from human-facing usefulness

Scope the time period and decision, then evaluate retrieval coverage separately from final-brief relevance and noise. A shared repository does not require identical retrieval or presentation for people and agents.

Attendee notes · S01
TAKEAWAY 03

Evaluate the work product people accept

For research, measure an accepted memo or useful signal. For advisors, measure a prepared meeting or accurate follow-up. Include the human corrections needed before use; a completed model response is only one step.

TAKEAWAY 04

Evaluate skill and memory changes on complete jobs

Define tasks and accepted completion before comparing bundles. Keep partial credit separate from binary pass criteria, and state the attempt budget and output-selection costs behind pass@k. Eight-to-four skills is an experiment, not a universal default.

Attendee notes · S07
TAKEAWAY 05

Calibrate judges as configured system components

Keep safety, task, and experience measures separate. Version the judge model and rubric; measure severe false passes, false failures, disagreement, and repeated-run variance. A fail-leaning judge can increase review burden.

Seek disconfirming cases. Test containment, detection, and human intervention alongside output quality; preserve found failures as regression cases and verify repairs on a separate assessment set. Frequency alone does not describe risk.

Connect the registry to enforced boundaries

Record owners, tools, endpoints, and resources, then test allowed and denied actions. For selective approval, measure missed consequential cases as well as unnecessary interruptions.
